# RawBoy Film

RawBoy Film is a raw camera app for Android and iOS whose looks are film stocks simulated with
[spektrafilm](https://github.com/andreavolpato/spektrafilm) by Andrea Volpato. It is RawBoy Camera with spektrafilm's
grain, halation and scatter, and installs next to it (`app.rawboy.camera.film`).

This repository is RawBoy Film's complete source: the RawBoy engine and camera it is built on, plus the film-only
files (`shaders/film/`, `app-camera/src/film/`, `ios/Camera/film/`, `ios/FilmEngineTests/`, `tools/spektrafilm_luts/`,
`tools/film_eval/`, `licenses/THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES_FILM.txt`). Third-party licences are in
[licenses/THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES_FILM.txt](licenses/THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES_FILM.txt), which the app shows in Settings.
LibRaw is used under the LGPL 2.1, and the Adobe DNG SDK is left out (its licence isn't GPL-compatible), so JPEG XL
DNGs don't open; the camera's own Pixel and iPhone DNGs go through LibRaw.

## Film looks

**RawBoy Film** is RawBoy Camera with film stocks as its looks (everything else above applies), simulated by
[spektrafilm](https://github.com/andreavolpato/spektrafilm) (Andrea Volpato): Yellow Portrait 400/800, Yellow Vivid 100, Yellow Warm 200,
Green Pastel 400, Green Everyday 400, Yellow Cine 250D and 500T (printed on cinema print film), and the slide
films Green Vivid Slide 100, Green Slide 100, Yellow Slide 100 and Yellow Classic 64 (scanned, not printed). Colour
negatives are printed on colour paper. Look names are RawBoy's own: "Yellow" and "Green" are two families of film
character, not products. **Natural** is the AgX develop alone. It saves to `Pictures/RawBoy Film` (`…-film.jpg`,
DNGs in `RAW/`), so its photos stay apart from RawBoy Camera's.

- The film replaces AgX: after white balance, exposure and Shadows/Highlights, the scene light exposes the negative,
  which is printed and the print scanned. spektrafilm computes that spectrally from the stocks' datasheets; RawBoy
  uses spektrafilm's own 2-LUT bake (film LUT: light → film density; print LUT: density → sRGB), generated by
  `tools/spektrafilm_luts/generate.py`.
- **Grain** is added between the two LUTs, in film density, with spektrafilm's particle statistics (more
  particles per pixel = finer grain; the amount follows density). It is sized on a 36 mm frame, so a downscaled
  photo shows it as a smaller scan would.
- **Halation** (light reflected back from the film base, the red glow around highlights) and in-emulsion
  **scatter** are spektrafilm's, sized in micrometres on the same 36 mm frame, so viewfinder and photo match.
- **Strength** blends the film render with the AgX one (grain and halation fade with it). Middle grey stays where
  AgX puts it, so exposure and Brightness behave as before. Whites reach the paper's white (about 0.85), not 1.0.

## Building

- Android: `./gradlew :app-camera:assembleFilmDebug` (`installFilmDebug` to install). NDK 27.2, CMake 3.22.1, JDK 21
  for the Gradle daemon. `:app-camera:assembleStandardDebug` builds plain RawBoy Camera from the same sources; it
  needs the Adobe DNG SDK in `third_party/dng_sdk` (git-ignored, see `third_party/README.md`), which the Film build
  never uses.
- iOS: `ios/native/build.sh`, then `cd ios && xcodegen`, then build the `RawBoyFilm` scheme
  (tests: `RawBoyFilmEngineTests`). See CLAUDE.md for the details, and [Installing on your iPhone](#installing-on-your-iphone)
  to put it on a phone.
- Film LUTs: `uv run tools/spektrafilm_luts/generate.py` regenerates `luts/spektrafilm/`.
- Releases: publishing a GitHub release runs `.github/workflows/release.yml`, which builds the signed Film APK
  (secrets `KEYSTORE_FILE`, `KEYSTORE_PASS`) and attaches it to the release.

## Installing on your iPhone

RawBoy Film isn't on the App Store. You build it on a Mac and install it on your own iPhone with Xcode, signed with
your Apple ID. The same project also builds plain RawBoy Camera (`RawBoyCamera` scheme), which installs next to it.

### What you need

- A Mac with **Xcode** (from the App Store; open it once so it installs its components) and an iPhone on **iOS 17**
  or newer, plus a USB cable for the first install.
- An **Apple ID**. A free one is enough; a paid Apple Developer Program membership ($99 a year) only changes how long
  an install lasts and how many apps you can have (see [Free or paid Apple ID](#free-or-paid-apple-id)).
- Build tools from [Homebrew](https://brew.sh): `brew install xcodegen cmake glslang spirv-cross`
- Network access for the first build: it downloads LibRaw, jpegli and MediaPipe. The Adobe DNG SDK is not needed
  for RawBoy Film.

### 1. Build the native parts

```sh
git clone https://github.com/lexusag/RawBoyFILM.git
cd RawBoyFILM
ios/native/build.sh        # LibRaw + jpegli → ios/native/out/*.xcframework, incl. RawBoyNativeFilm (slow the first time)
```

### 2. Set your signing team

The Xcode project is generated from `ios/project.yml`, which is signed with the maintainer's team
(`DEVELOPMENT_TEAM: PB2FFKSMC8`) and uses `app.rawboy.*` bundle ids. If that's your team, skip to step 3. Otherwise:

1. In Xcode, **Settings → Accounts → +** and sign in with your Apple ID. This creates your *Personal Team* (free) or
   shows your paid team.
2. Find your Team ID: Xcode makes an "Apple Development" certificate the first time it signs, and its OU field is the
   team ID. Either open any project, pick your team under *Signing & Capabilities* once and read *Development Team*
   in Build Settings, or run:
   ```sh
   security find-certificate -c "Apple Development" -p | openssl x509 -noout -subject
   ```
3. Put your team in `ios/project.yml` and give the apps bundle ids of your own (a bundle id belongs to the first team
   that registers it, so `app.rawboy.*` won't sign under another team):
   ```sh
   cd ios
   sed -i '' 's/PB2FFKSMC8/YOURTEAMID/; s/app\.rawboy/com.yourname.rawboy/g' project.yml
   ```
   Keep this as a local change (or `git stash` it when you pull). Don't set the team in Xcode's Signing tab instead:
   `xcodegen` regenerates the project and drops it.

### 3. Generate the project and install

```sh
cd ios
xcodegen                   # also generates the Metal shaders and downloads MediaPipe
open RawBoy.xcodeproj
```

1. Connect the iPhone with the cable, unlock it and tap **Trust** on "Trust This Computer?".
2. Turn on **Developer Mode** on the iPhone: **Settings → Privacy & Security → Developer Mode**, then restart and
   confirm. The switch only appears after the iPhone has been connected to Xcode once.
3. In Xcode, pick the **RawBoyFilm** scheme and your iPhone as the run destination at the top of the window, then
   **Product → Run** (⌘R). The Debug build is what goes on the phone; the engine is compiled optimised in Debug too.
4. With a free Apple ID, the first launch is refused as an "Untrusted Developer". On the iPhone, open **Settings →
   General → VPN & Device Management**, tap your Apple ID under *Developer App* and **Trust** it. Then open RawBoy
   Film from the home screen and allow the camera and Photos when asked.

After the first install you can tick **Connect via network** for the iPhone in **Window → Devices and Simulators**
and install over Wi-Fi without the cable. From the command line instead of the Xcode window (the device id comes from
`xcrun devicectl list devices`):

```sh
xcodebuild -project RawBoy.xcodeproj -scheme RawBoyFilm -configuration Debug \
    -destination 'generic/platform=iOS' -derivedDataPath build/dd -allowProvisioningUpdates build
xcrun devicectl device install app --device <device id> build/dd/Build/Products/Debug-iphoneos/RawBoyFilm.app
```

### Free or paid Apple ID

| | Free Apple ID (Personal Team) | Apple Developer Program |
|---|---|---|
| Install lasts | **7 days**, then the app won't open until you reinstall it | 1 year |
| Apps on one iPhone | **3** at a time (with RawBoy's own apps installed too, remove one to make room) | no limit |
| New bundle ids | 10 per 7 days | no limit |

When an install expires, just run it again from Xcode (step 3): with the same bundle id it updates in place and keeps
the app's settings. Photos already saved to your library stay there even if you delete the app. Updating is the
same: `git pull`, `ios/native/build.sh` if `engine/src/main/cpp` or `ios/native` changed, `xcodegen`, Run.

### When it doesn't work

- **"Failed to register bundle identifier" / "No profiles for 'app.rawboy…' were found"**: the bundle ids still belong
  to another team. Change them as in step 2.
- **"Your maximum App ID limit has been reached"**: the free 10-per-week limit. Wait, or reuse the bundle ids you
  already registered rather than inventing new ones.
- **"The maximum number of apps for free development profiles has been reached"**: three apps are already installed
  with your free team. Delete one from the iPhone.
- **"Could not launch… Developer Mode disabled"**: step 3.2. **"Untrusted Developer"**: step 3.4.
- **`glslang: command not found` or missing `.metal` files**: the Homebrew tools aren't installed.
- **"No such module 'RawBoyNativeFilm'" / missing xcframework**: run `ios/native/build.sh` first, then `xcodegen` again.
- **`xcrun: unable to find utility` / no iOS SDK**: `xcode-select` points at the Command Line Tools. Run
  `sudo xcode-select -s /Applications/Xcode.app/Contents/Developer`.

## Keeping up with RawBoy

The engine and camera are developed in RawBoy's own repository. `tools/sync_from_rawboy.sh <path to a RawBoy
checkout>` copies the shared files from it and leaves the film-only files, this README, the iOS project, the release
workflow and the Gradle settings alone. Review the diff, build, and commit.
